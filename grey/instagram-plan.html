Setting up @grey before the first drop

How to set up Grey's Instagram, what to post for the first month, and who to work with. Built from the Grey marketing brief, the social and influencer-marketing skills, and research on the brands and creators you named.

1 · Where Grey sits

Cole Henry's weight, Angel Boy's tailoring, one colour

Joshua Mohamed

New York, since 2020. Cropped hoodies, wide-leg and flare sweats, embroidered JM logos. Hoodies about $100 to $125.

Take: the relaxed bottoms. Leave: the logo.

Cole Henry

Heavyweight boxy "blanks" (400 GSM fleece), monthly drops. Blank hoodie $70, zip $80.

Take: fabric weight and boxy fit.

Angel Boy

Sydney, since 2024, by Will Stokoe. "Formal streetwear": pinstripe and pleated trousers, henleys. Trousers $179 to $229.

Take: one dressed-up piece per drop (Grey's pleated trouser).

Grey's line in one sentence: heavyweight basics and one tailored piece per drop, all in four greys, with no logo on the chest. None of the three owns a single colour; that is the gap.

4 · The look of the feed

Rules that keep the grid looking like one brand

Colour and grade

Only the four greys plus skin, denim and wood. One preset on every photo: low contrast, slightly warm, no heavy film grain. Shoot outdoors at golden hour or by a window.

What's in the photos

Full-length fits with the legs and shoes in shot, mirror fit checks, close-ups of fabric and seams, flat lays on concrete or a white sheet. Faces optional; posture and fit matter more.

Captions

Short, lowercase allowed, one fact per caption: the piece, the fit or the date. 3 to 5 hashtags at most. No em dashes, no "Agree?", no emoji bullets.

7 · Creators

How to work with creators at Grey's stage

Start with gifting to micro creators

Send 10 to 20 micro creators (10k to 50k followers) a free piece with no obligation to post. Micro creators convert better per follower than big accounts and cost product, not fees. Paid posts at this tier run roughly $100 to $1,500 each.

Write to them one by one

Name a specific post of theirs and why Grey fits it. Ask their size. One short message, one follow-up a week later, then stop.

Required, every time

Any free product or payment must be disclosed: "#ad" or "gifted" where people see it, plus Instagram's paid-partnership label. Give each creator their own code (e.g. ANTON10) to measure sales. If you pay, buy the right to reuse their video as an ad for 3 to 6 months.
