# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v2
**Last updated:** 2026-11-27

> Drafted from the founder's first brief. Everything marked *(assumed)* is a working default to confirm.

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Grey is a men's clothing brand of heavyweight, quiet basics in shades of grey.
**What it does:** Makes a small range of everyday pieces (tees, hoodies, sweatpants) with boxy, relaxed fits and no loud branding, released in limited drops. *(assumed: first drop is basics)*
**Product category:** Men's streetwear basics / minimal menswear.
**Product type:** E-commerce, direct to consumer, drop model.
**Business model:** Limited drops sold online; pre-launch waitlist first. Prices not set yet.

## Target Audience
**Who:** Men roughly 18–28 who follow the clean, muted Instagram menswear look (creators like antownnn, masonwkass, caficosta_, ethan_kieffer, braydenstiles).
**Primary use case:** A small wardrobe of neutral pieces that look put-together with no effort, in photos and in person.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Look like the creators they follow without buying a full designer outfit
- Own a few basics that fit right (boxy top, relaxed leg) and go with everything
- Wear something that signals taste without a big logo
**Use cases:**
- Everyday outfit, campus, gym-to-street
- Outfit photos and mirror shots for their own Instagram

## Personas
B2C; one persona: the 20-year-old who saves outfit posts and builds his wardrobe from them.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** The look he wants is built from expensive designer pieces or from basics that fit badly.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Fast-fashion basics are thin and lose shape after a few washes
- Designer basics cost a week's pay for a plain tee
- Most streetwear brands lead with a loud logo
**Emotional tension:** Wants to look effortless; doesn't want to look like he's trying.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct (the founder's references):**
- Joshua Mohamed (New York, est. 2020): cropped hoodies, wide-leg and flare sweats, JM logo embroidery; hoodies ~$100–125. Falls short for us: logo-led, many colours.
- Cole Henry (US): heavyweight boxy "blanks", 400 GSM fleece; blank hoodie $70, zip $80; monthly drops. Closest on fabric and fit; no single colour story.
- Angel Boy (Sydney, est. 2024, Will Stokoe): "formal streetwear", pinstripe and pleated trousers, henleys; trousers $179–229. Closest on the dressed-up side; pricier.
**Gap Grey takes:** Cole Henry's weight and fit + Angel Boy's dressed-down tailoring, in one grey palette with no logo.
**Secondary:** Big-brand basics (heavyweight tees from mainstream labels): good fabric, no point of view.
**Indirect:** Thrifting and vintage: unique, but hit-or-miss on fit.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- One colour story: everything is a shade of grey, so every piece goes with every other
- Fit first: boxy tops and relaxed bottoms cut for the current silhouette
- Quiet branding: no chest logo
**Why customers choose us:** The look of the feed, in pieces that last, at a price below designer.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "It's just a grey tee." | Show the weight, the cut and the fit on real bodies; let the fabric close-ups do the work. |
| "New brand, will it fit?" | Size guide with garment measurements; fit photos on several builds. |
| "Will it sell out / is it worth waiting for?" | Waitlist gets first access and the drop date. |

**Anti-persona:** Someone who wants bright colour, big graphics or logo-led hype.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Basics that shrink and twist; logos he's tired of.
**Pull:** A coherent grey wardrobe that photographs well.
**Habit:** Buying whatever the big brands drop.
**Anxiety:** Fit and quality from a new label.

## Customer Language
**Words to use:** heavyweight, boxy, relaxed, washed, grey, drop, fit, everyday
**Words to avoid:** luxury, elevated, premium (unproven), essentials (overused), any hype words
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Drop | A limited release on a set date |
| Waitlist | Email list that gets first access |

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Quiet | Short lines, lower case allowed on social, few words | Mysterious for its own sake | "Grey. Drop one soon." |
| Exact | Fabric weights, fits, dates stated plainly | Spec-sheet coldness | "Boxy tee, heavyweight cotton, dropped shoulder." |
| Confident | States, doesn't sell | Arrogant, hype | "Made to be worn every day." |

**Tone by context:**
- Social: few words, no emoji bullets, no hype
- Launch: the date, the pieces, the link
**Never use:** "elevate", "luxury", "drip", fake scarcity

## Proof Points
None yet (pre-launch). Do not invent reviews, numbers or customers.

## Goals
**Business goal:** Build a following and a waitlist before the first drop.
**Conversion action:** Join the waitlist (website) / follow and turn on notifications (Instagram).
