Drop one. Four pieces you can wear together.

  • Boxy teeTops

    Heavyweight cotton jersey, dropped shoulder, cut to sit at the hip.

  • HoodieTops

    Heavy cotton fleece, a boxy body and a double-layer hood, with no chest logo.

  • SweatpantBottoms

    Relaxed straight leg with an open hem and deep side pockets.

  • Pleated trouserBottoms

    Single pleat and a wide leg, worn with the tee or under a knit.

Four greys. Every piece comes in each of them.

Ash

A light grey, close to washed concrete.

Stone

A warm mid grey with a little brown in it.

Slate

A cool darker grey with a hint of blue.

Graphite

Almost black, softer than black in daylight.

Why grey

One colour in four shades, so every piece goes with every other piece. Fewer clothes, worn more often, with nothing printed across the chest.
