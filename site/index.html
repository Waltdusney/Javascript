Three modes for three questions.

daily

What's moving today?

The full pre-market brief: regime, futures, gappers with catalysts, today's reporters and a watchlist.

week

What's coming this week?

Earnings and macro releases day by day, with ET times, holidays and early closes.

NVDA AMD

What about my names?

For each ticker: latest dated news, upcoming catalysts, the prior session's move and levels cited by sources.

Built to admit what it doesn't know.

Search results often mix up dates. The scanner checks every figure before it goes in the brief, and if it can't confirm something, it says so instead of guessing.

  1. 01

    Pin the date

    Works out the previous trading session, NYSE holidays and whether it's pre-market or after hours.

  2. 02

    Research in parallel

    Runs dated searches for closes, futures, movers, earnings, the macro calendar and breaking news.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Checks every figure's date, cross-checks index closes across sources and marks rumors as unconfirmed.

  4. 04

    Write the brief

    Puts the regime and watchlist first, then lists its gaps and links every source.
